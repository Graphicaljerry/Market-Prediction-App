# Design Context

> Used by the design skills (impeccable, typeset, layout, clarify, polish, audit, critique, …).
> Keep this accurate — it's the source of truth for design decisions.

## Users
A solo crypto bettor (the owner) placing **15-minute Over/Under bets on Kalshi/Coinbase**.
Used in short, focused bursts on **phone (primary) and desktop**, often under mild time pressure
right before a round locks. The job: *glance, understand the call + how confident to be, act fast.*

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, trustworthy, quietly high-end. Three words: **clear, composed, confident.**
It should feel like an Apple-grade instrument — not a flashy "crypto" dashboard.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Dark theme** (fast trading context; long sessions). Optional Apple-style "liquid glass" skin,
  capability-detected so it only shows where supported.
- **Few colours, used sparingly.** The ONE loud accent is the live status colour that flips
  green (OVER) / red (UNDER) / amber (skip) with the pick. Per-provider AI badge is the only other
  hue. Everything else is neutral on near-black. 60-30-10 weighting.
- **Skimmable.** Numbers and the call are the heroes; prose is secondary and tucked/collapsible.
- Anti-references: generic AI dashboards, AI-purple/blue gradients, neon-on-dark, busy multi-colour.

## Design Principles
1. **The pick is sacred.** Visual changes never alter pick logic. Accuracy > tidiness.
2. **Fewer colours, more hierarchy.** Reach for weight, size, and space before colour.
3. **Calm motion.** Smooth ease-out only — no bounce/elastic. Honour reduced-motion.
4. **Honest, plain language.** Short, active, no jargon; explain *what to do* and *why* in layman's terms.
5. **No AI-slop tells.** No gradient text, no side-stripe accent borders, no card-in-card, no
   decorative sparklines, no centered-everything.

## Type
- **Geist** (UI sans, SF-adjacent) + **Geist Mono** (numbers, timer, data). Tabular figures for
  anything that should align. Replaced Inter + Space Mono (overused defaults).
